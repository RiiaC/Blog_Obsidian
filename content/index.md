# Welcome to my blog
After years of using Dreamwidth, and before that LiveJournal, I am in the process of moving myself here. Why? Because I fell in love with the note taking program, [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md/) last year and have transitioned all of my writing there. When I found out that it is possible to turn an Obsidian vault into a web page, I decided to give it a try.

I got it working on 2025-12-02, but at that point, only 2025’s blog posts plus unpublished notes for days that never made it to my Dreamwidth page. However, I would like to eventually go back and import all of older Dreamwidth posts, eventually. In the meantime, anyone who is curious can [head over there to look.](https://kareina.dreamwidth.org/archive)

In addition to the navigation bar on the left, you can use the graph view to navigate by zooming in clicking on entries there.
