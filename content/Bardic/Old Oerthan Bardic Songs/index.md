---
title: Old Oerthan Bardic Songs
---
When I first joined the SCA, in the 1980's, in the Principality of Oertha, we had a very strong Bardic tradition, with singing in the evenings at every event, and regular weekly gatherings for singing practice.

Some few of those bardics were recorded to tapes, and I listened to them often through the 1990's and even into the early 2000s. Some time along the way I shared copies of the tapes with Thorvald Grimson, who digitised them. Therefore this folder is to test and see if it is possible to share any of those recordings by hosting them in my google drive and linking to them here.
