Plan: Create a small room in the attic with a toilet and sink, running the plumbing down next to the ventilation that goes between the bathroom on the main floor and the linen closet. In the cellar, add an additional toilet inn the laundry room, connecting with the same new plumbing. This will transform the house from having one toilet to having three!

The work to start week four of 2026, with [Robertsfors byggkonsult ](https://www.robertsforsbyggkonsult.se/) doing all of the prep work to make the room ready, and [BVS Rör](https://www.bvsror.se/) doing all the plumbing and installation of toilets and sinks.
