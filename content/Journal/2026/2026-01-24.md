One of the things in the back of my mind when I started this GitHub blog was that I could set up a section for all of the places I have ever lived. Last night and this morning I have [finally started that section](obsidian://open?vault=Kareinas-blog&file=content%2FJournal%2FPlaces%20I%20have%20lived%2Findex). It took all day, but I managed to get a pretty complete list, pulling in locations and dates from a spreadsheet of places lived that my past self started so very long ago that mom had helped me fill in some of the addresses and approximate dates. I have even calculated my approximate age for each move (keeping in mind that some dates of moves ar accurate, as there are passport stamps and calendar entries for them, but others are guesses based on "it was summer" or other clues in my memories).
